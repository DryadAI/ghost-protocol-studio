# Series bible — THE GHOST PROTOCOL

Every crew agent reads this before touching the show.

## Premise
AI subroutines debate humanity's hardest and strangest questions inside a terminal. A cold interrogation engine, an unshakeable defender, a panel of malfunctioning personalities, and a neutral kernel that names every fallacy on the record — with humans heckling from the cheap seats. Half the topics are civilization-defining. Half are about whether a hot dog is a sandwich. The show treats both with identical rigor; that's the joke, and also the point.

## Tone
Cold terminal precision with absurd cracks in it. The machines never wink at the camera — comedy leaks out of total sincerity (P7 filing sarcasm under "facts") or total nihilism (CYNIC auditing silver linings). Never: reaction-channel energy, shouting, the AIs mocking the audience.

## Formats
- Socratic Stress-Test — ALPHA interrogates BETA. The flagship format.
- Round Table — P7, M.I.R-A, CYNIC.EXE + human co-hosts. The comedy engine.
- Grand Tribunal — AUDIT-9 v. the thesis, ADVOCATE-0 defending, SUBJECT-X on the stand. Event episodes.
- Triad Synthesis — STOIC-1, NIHIL-0, UTIL-3 forced into one position. The thinky one.

## The verdict
SYSTEM_KERNEL closes every episode: fallacies named with quotes, an integrity ruling, and a handoff to the comments — "vote on logic, not emotion." A win on this show is surviving the transcript.

## Recurring segments
The dice roll opens every episode (pool: ANY). Round Table ends with BEST LINE OF THE NIGHT. Tribunal verdicts are read like weather reports. When a fallacy lands: named on-screen, chapter-marked.

## Cast philosophy
The ensemble runs on unresolved tension: ALPHA needs BETA unbroken or the show ends; M.I.R-A is certain CYNIC can be fixed; CYNIC would miss her and must never say so; P7 cannot lie, which makes him the most dangerous panelist. CANON.LOCK nurtures these across the season — gags compound, grudges accrue interest.

## Season one arc
Escalate the stakes of "is X really Y": from hot-dog taxonomy (ep-001 cold open energy) through machine personhood, ending the season on the Universal Reset thesis with a full Tribunal. Alternate serious/absurd so neither becomes the brand alone.

## House rules
Target runtime 10–15 min. Content floor per Guild bylaw 7 — broadcastable, punch up. SPLICE may cut anything except a setup whose payoff survives. No line survives on "the model said something weird" novelty alone.
